New paper was published in ACS Omega

My new paper Discovery of Vascular Endothelial Growth Factor Receptor 2 Inhibitors Employing Junction Tree Variational Autoencoder with Bayesian Optimization and Gradient Ascent was published on 2024-11-11 in the ACS Omega journal.

Huge thanks to all collaborators on this work including Thanh-An Pham, Van-Thinh To, Hoang-Son Lai Le, Phuoc-Chung Van Nguyen, The-Chuong Trinh, Tieu-Long Phan and Assoc. Prof. Tuyen Ngoc Truong. I would also like to especially thank Phan Tieu Long and Truong Ngoc Tuyen for their supervision, guidance, and support throughout this work. I also send my deepest gratitude to KOICA for enabling me to complete this research.
